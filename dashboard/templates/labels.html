Source labels

DRBR measures brain-rot content only. Label each source by asking: is this content I consume? If yes, is it brain rot (wasteful) or not (worthwhile)? If I only go there to get a task done — buy, pay, book, log in, organise, check the weather — it's a tool and isn't counted.

You decide what each source is. An unlabelled source counts as wasteful until you label it. Tool minutes are left out of every wasteful and worthwhile figure and shown on their own. Changing a label re-records each week's overage immediately.
